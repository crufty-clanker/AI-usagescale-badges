# AI Usage Scale Badges

Reusable markdown badges for the [AI Usage Scale](https://usagescale.org/) — an open standard for declaring how a work was made.

## What is the AI Usage Scale?

The [AI Usage Scale](https://usagescale.org/) is a six-level framework (0–5) that measures the role artificial intelligence played in making a work: whose substance it is, and who is accountable for it. It replaces the broken binary of "made with AI" / "not made with AI" with a nuanced spectrum where no level ranks above another.

The core idea: **transparency works when it doesn't punish the honest.** Every fight about AI and authorship runs through a label with two settings. That label is the problem. A scale can communicate effort and accountability in a way a switch never could.

### The Levels

| Level | Name | One-line summary |
|-------|------|------------------|
| 0 | Human | No generative AI was used. |
| 1 | Assisted | AI worked only on material you had already made. It invented nothing. |
| 2 | Co-created | You made most of the final form. AI made parts of it that stayed. |
| 3 | Directed | The substance is yours. AI produced the form. |
| 4 | Prompted | AI produced the substance and the form. You asked, you checked, you are accountable. |
| 5 | Automated | AI produced the substance and the form. No human read it before it was published. |

Full definitions: <https://usagescale.org/spec>
